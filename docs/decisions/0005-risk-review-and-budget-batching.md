# Review critical boundaries and avoid standalone budget turns

The complete [three-task cohort](../../benchmarks/stream-suite-20261024-01/README.md)
failed its preregistered objective. All six branches completed, all 147 native
requests completed semantically, and all six artifacts were accepted. Tree scored
98/96; Ledger and SafeZip scored 100/100 each. Flow used more native requests on
every task and was slower on the two deterministic CLI tasks.

## Evidence and diagnosis

Tree Flow lost one existing safe-projection point for incomplete nonfinite-value
defense and one subjective aesthetic point. Its ordinary complete-model geometry
and all 57 native controls passed. Telemetry does **not** identify who authored
the affected code: the defect cannot honestly be attributed to the medium helper.
The coordination contract nevertheless encouraged implementation proposals from
a medium worker without explicitly keeping critical correctness boundaries under
the chief's ownership and verification.

The [Tree runtime audit](../evidence/tree-case09-runtime-audit.json) records six
MCP calls taking 0.234 seconds in total. They were already combined with other
actions between model requests; they are not six proven extra inference turns.
Ledger had four MCP calls, including two isolated late status stages. Their
producing model requests took 6.860 and 4.078 seconds; the next requests took
2.829 and 13.703 seconds. These are observations, **not counterfactual savings**.
The 57.485-second Ledger difference cannot be assigned wholly to status tools.
Helper and chief intervals overlap and must not be added as elapsed time.

## Changed policy

1. The first helper uses the existing `reviewer/high` profile. It returns at most
   five concrete acceptance risks and boundary checks from the supplied task.
   The initial pass performs no tools, file reads, polling or production coding;
   the chief implements concurrently.
2. The chief owns numerical transformations, parsing/input validation, security
   decisions and atomic/transactional boundaries. It tests the actual production
   path with applicable finite/nonfinite, zero/near-zero, malformed and empty
   cases. Medium implementation proposals remain available for independent,
   low-risk modules with stable APIs.
3. Only the chief queries budget status, normally at most twice: the first
   runnable slice and before final verification. It batches status with already
   necessary independent work when supported and skips post-acceptance status.
   An explicit verification stress protocol may require more calls.
4. When rendered output is required, the chief inspects a real representative
   artifact for clipping and legibility against the stated acceptance bar.

These are instructions, not new hard enforcement or a guaranteed quality/speed
improvement. The reviewer may cost more per request; fewer calls and a higher
profile do not establish lower subscription usage. Parent permissions, providers,
request bodies, the terminal-aware reader, deadlines, request limits and Harness
guard remain unchanged. No installed global skill/configuration is edited.

## Prospective confirmation

Run one new native prerequisite and one separately preregistered Tree A/B, keeping
the literal task, evaluator/rubric, 57-check protocol, Sol XHigh roots and
1500-second/80-request limits. Verify a real parent-linked high-profile child and
observed budget-call counts. A policy deviation remains visible. A fresh blind
judge scores untouched sources after generation. Both branches must complete and
be accepted, Flow quality must be at least Solo quality, and the preregistered
time ratio must reach 1.10. The original negative denominator-three cohort remains
failed regardless of the new result. No unchanged retry-until-win is allowed.
